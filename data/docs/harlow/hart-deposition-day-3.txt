Harlow Orchards v. Brightline Freight, CGC-26-418822
Deposition of Caleb Hart (Brightline scheduler), Day 3. Notes by M. Chen.

Started at 10:20, twenty minutes late because the court reporter's laptop wouldn't connect. Opposing counsel (Alan Pierce) objected to form on nearly every question. None of it matters.

Hart admitted the refrigeration unit on trailer 4451 showed a temperature alarm at 02:05 on 13 Jul, and that nobody on the night shift acknowledged it until 07:30. The witness said the alarm log "gets ignored a lot" because of false alarms.

Hart confirmed Brightline's policy requires a driver call within 30 minutes of any alarm, and could not name anyone who made that call.

Hart estimated the load at 1,269 cases of pears for Harlow Orchards, and said Brightline's claims team valued the loss at 40,900. Ours is 58,500. Flag for the damages expert.

On the email chain, Hart said the supervisor, Joan Ellis, told the witness "don't write anything down about the alarm." Pierce instructed the witness not to answer follow-ups. We should move to compel.

We broke twice for phone calls. The last hour covered background we already knew.
