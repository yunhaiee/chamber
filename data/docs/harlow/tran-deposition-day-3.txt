Harlow Orchards v. Brightline Freight, CGC-26-418822
Deposition of Owen Tran (Brightline mechanic), Day 3. Notes by M. Chen.

Started at 10:40, ten minutes late because the exhibits binder was left at the office. Opposing counsel (Alan Pierce) objected to form on nearly every question. None of it matters.

Tran admitted the refrigeration unit on trailer 4345 showed a temperature alarm at 02:15 on August 16, and that nobody on the night shift acknowledged it until 05:00. The witness said the alarm log "gets ignored a lot" because of false alarms.

Tran confirmed Brightline's policy requires a driver call within 30 minutes of any alarm, and could not name anyone who made that call.

Tran estimated the load at 1,001 cases of nectarines for Harlow Orchards, and said Brightline's claims team valued the loss at 45,200. Ours is 60,000. Flag for the damages expert.

On the email chain, Tran said a supervisor, Lena Park, said "don't write anything down about the alarm." Pierce instructed the witness not to answer follow-ups. We should move to compel.

The room was freezing and everyone complained. Most of the afternoon repeated the morning.
