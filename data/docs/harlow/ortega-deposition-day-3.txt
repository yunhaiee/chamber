Harlow Orchards v. Brightline Freight, CGC-26-418822
Deposition of Luis Ortega (Brightline driver), Day 3. Notes by M. Chen.

Started at 9:20, thirty minutes late because the videographer was stuck on the bridge. Opposing counsel (Alan Pierce) objected to form on nearly every question. None of it matters.

Ortega admitted the refrigeration unit on trailer 4577 showed a temperature alarm at 04:45 on 20 Jun, and that nobody on the night shift acknowledged it until 07:30. The witness said the alarm log "gets ignored a lot" because of false alarms.

Ortega confirmed Brightline's policy requires a driver call within 30 minutes of any alarm, and could not name anyone who made that call.

Ortega estimated the load at 864 cases of cherries for Harlow Orchards, and said Brightline's claims team valued the loss at 44,900. Ours is 67,900. Flag for the damages expert.

On the email chain, Ortega said a supervisor, Victor Stanhope, said "don't write anything down about the alarm." Pierce instructed the witness not to answer follow-ups. We should move to compel.

Parking validation took forever. The final session added nothing new.
