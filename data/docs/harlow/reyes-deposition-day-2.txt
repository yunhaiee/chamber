PRIVILEGED & CONFIDENTIAL · ATTORNEY WORK PRODUCT
Harlow Orchards v. Brightline Freight, CGC-26-418822
Deposition of Tom Reyes (Brightline dispatcher), Day 2. Notes by M. Chen.

Started at 9:40, twenty minutes late because the court reporter's laptop wouldn't connect. Opposing counsel (Alan Pierce) objected to form on nearly every question. None of it matters.

Reyes admitted the refrigeration unit on trailer 4471 showed a temperature alarm at 02:15 on 14 August, and that nobody on the night shift acknowledged it until 06:30. He said the alarm log "gets ignored a lot" because of false alarms.

He confirmed Brightline's policy requires a driver call within 30 minutes of any alarm. He could not name anyone who made that call.

He estimated the load at 1,180 cases of Harlow pears, and said Brightline's claims team valued the loss at 61,400. Ours is 74,900. Flag for the damages expert.

On the email chain, he said his supervisor, Karen Doyle, told him "don't write anything down about the alarm." Pierce instructed him not to answer follow-ups. We should move to compel.

Lunch ran long. The afternoon was mostly exhibits we already had.
