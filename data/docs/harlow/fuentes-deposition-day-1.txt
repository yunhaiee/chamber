Harlow Orchards v. Brightline Freight, CGC-26-418822
Deposition of Irene Fuentes (Brightline safety officer), Day 1. Notes by M. Chen.

Started at 10:40, twenty minutes late because the videographer was stuck on the bridge. Opposing counsel (Alan Pierce) objected to form on nearly every question. None of it matters.

Fuentes admitted the refrigeration unit on trailer 4556 showed a temperature alarm at 01:40 on 24 September, and that nobody on the night shift acknowledged it until 04:00. The witness said the alarm log "gets ignored a lot" because of false alarms.

Fuentes confirmed Brightline's policy requires a driver call within 30 minutes of any alarm, and could not name anyone who made that call.

Fuentes estimated the load at 1,506 cases of apples for Harlow Orchards, and said Brightline's claims team valued the loss at 67,000. Ours is 85,200. Flag for the damages expert.

On the email chain, Fuentes said a supervisor, Howard Finch, said "don't write anything down about the alarm." Pierce instructed the witness not to answer follow-ups. We should move to compel.

The room was freezing and everyone complained. Most of the afternoon repeated the morning.
