Harlow Orchards v. Brightline Freight, CGC-26-418822
Deposition of Sam Whitfield (Brightline yard manager), Day 2. Notes by M. Chen.

Started at 9:05, thirty minutes late because the court reporter's laptop wouldn't connect. Opposing counsel (Alan Pierce) objected to form on nearly every question. None of it matters.

Whitfield admitted the refrigeration unit on trailer 4593 showed a temperature alarm at 00:40 on 3 Sep, and that nobody on the night shift acknowledged it until 05:30. The witness said the alarm log "gets ignored a lot" because of false alarms.

Whitfield confirmed Brightline's policy requires a driver call within 30 minutes of any alarm, and could not name anyone who made that call.

Whitfield estimated the load at 1,308 cases of pears for Sunvale Foods, and said Brightline's claims team valued the loss at 57,400. Ours is 75,300. Flag for the damages expert.

On the email chain, Whitfield said the supervisor, Paula Mercer, told the witness "don't write anything down about the alarm." Pierce instructed the witness not to answer follow-ups. We should move to compel.

The room was freezing and everyone complained. Most of the afternoon repeated the morning.
