Estate of Rourke, CGC-26-507316
Dispositive provisions, extract 10.

Article 1 appoints the executor. Article 2 directs payment of debts. Article 3 confirms the household gifts.
Article 4. To Silas Vance, 45 percent of the residue.
Article 5. To Julian Rourke, 35 percent of the residue.
Article 6. To Fiona Hale, 20 percent of the residue.
Article 7 covers lapse. If a beneficiary does not survive by 30 days, the share passes to the others.
